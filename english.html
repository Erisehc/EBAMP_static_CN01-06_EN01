EBAMP candidate 01: evidence and interpretation

A balanced configuration combining functional persistence and a posterior-mover illustration.

How to interpret the statistics

Within-fit BH is the primary display. It adjusts the GO tests within a fitted configuration. Pooled BH combines the tested configurations and is shown as an optional sensitivity analysis, not a universally required correction or a robustness score.

Manually specifying parameters does not itself require pooled correction. Selecting a configuration after inspecting its GO results introduces selection effects. Pooled BH is one exploratory diagnostic and does not fully correct the adaptive analysis or provide independent validation. Coverage: 287 community-GO fits and 20 mover-GO fits; these are not all 2,196 structural fits.

This candidate uses K=10, BA-SoS initialization and seed 0. Layers are pseudotime bins. GO enrichment measures annotation overrepresentation, not biological activity. Neither GO nor co-clustering establishes causal TF regulation.

Definitions and limitations

Hard movers change aligned most-likely labels; posterior movers have model P(switch)>0.5; intersection requires both. Posterior movers need not be a subset of hard movers. A smaller enrichment q-value is not a measured improvement in switching accuracy.

Core is the intersection of GO-hit genes assigned to a fixed cluster across all 12 layers; union includes every such gene appearing there. Persistent enrichment can coexist with an empty core. No candidate has pooled-significant TF-membership enrichment; TF-target enrichment is a separate question.

These examples were selected after examining results. Different GO terms and transitions are illustrated across candidates, so their q-values do not form a common performance ranking. Independent or resampling validation remains necessary.
